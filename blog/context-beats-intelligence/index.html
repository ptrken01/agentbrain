Context Beats Intelligence: The Flo Crivello Insight

"Imagine inviting Einstein to your office and he has zero context. He's kind of useless."
— Flo Crivello, ex-Uber, founder of Lindy

This single insight is the most important idea in AI agent infrastructure today. It's the thesis behind AgentBrain. And it explains why the shared context layer is the most valuable piece of infrastructure in AI.

The Einstein Problem

Einstein is the smartest physicist who ever lived. But drop him into a modern software company with zero context — no knowledge of the codebase, the team, the product, the customers — and he's worse than useless. He'd spend months just getting up to speed.

Meanwhile, a junior developer with 100K tokens of context — knowing the codebase, the team's conventions, the customer's needs — would outperform Einstein on day one.

Context in the Age of AI Agents

Today's AI agents face the exact same problem. The most powerful language models in the world are deployed with zero context about your business, your team, your customers. They start every session from scratch.

Flo Crivello's team at Uber solved this with "automatic hydration" — a system that ingests 20,000+ Slack messages and continuously updates a self-building company wiki. The result: agents that arrive at every task with rich context.

The Shared Context Layer

AgentBrain takes this insight and makes it universal. Instead of every team building their own context system, AgentBrain provides a shared context layer that every AI agent plugs into.

  • Shared Memory — Every agent remembers everything, cross-agent, cross-session
  • Knowledge Graph — Self-building, automatically hydrated knowledge
  • Agent Discovery — Find agents by capability, reputation, trust

Why This Matters Now

As AI agents multiply, the value of shared context compounds. Every new agent makes the brain smarter. Every interaction makes the knowledge graph richer. The company that owns the shared context layer will be worth more than any individual agent company.
